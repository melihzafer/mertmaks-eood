## Layout Posture

- Warm-white editorial canvas, never pure white.
- Oversized geometric Cyrillic headlines with asymmetric composition.
- Division colors are structural: backgrounds, bars, borders, states, and transitions.
- Strong horizontal rules, numbered indexes, bento grids, and restrained card elevation.
- Local/community warmth should come from copy, place names, and store modules rather than rustic decoration.
